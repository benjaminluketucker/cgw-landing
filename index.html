Inside the app

What you’ll find.

Three places to spend your week.

01

The Weekly Edit.

A new edition every week. Cool Girl News for what is actually happening, Brand Finds for the labels worth knowing, and Outfit Ideas with every piece linked to the brand.

02

Discover and Shop the Feed.

Shop the Feed is the running feed of brand finds, style guides and shopping guides. Discover is where all of it lives, filtered by source and by type so you can find the one you remember.

03

Saved.

Save any article or post as you read it. Your saved list keeps articles and posts apart, so the piece you wanted to come back to is where you left it.
